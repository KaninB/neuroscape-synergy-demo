How the numbers are calculated. Turn curves come from n = 5,000 simulated structural games per position — the percent is the share of games where the tier first came online by that turn (Wilson 95% range on hover). Opening-hand and RAM figures come from the same games. The model omits card effects, tokens, attacks, opponent interaction, and lethal endings.
