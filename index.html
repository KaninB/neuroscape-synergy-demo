    Structural model. Turn estimates omit printed card effects, tokens, attacks, opponent interaction, and lethal endings. The heuristic may force quit characters. Your deck stays in this browser.
    How this works. For any legal deck (50–255 cards) the tool simulates 5,000 structural games per position — the simulated player installs RAM, draws, deploys characters and gear, and clicks each Mainframe tier the moment its printed requirement is on the table. Every percent on a curve is the share of those games where the tier first came online by that turn (± standard error on hover). The opening-hand figures are exact combinatorics, not simulation. The model omits card effects, tokens, attacks, opponent interaction, and lethal endings — treat the curves as honest estimates of deck structure, not a crystal ball.
    Formulas
    P(reached by turn t) ~ simulated games with first hit by t / trials
    standard error = √(p(1−p)/n)
    Opening-hand mean = 5 × sources / deck size; qualify share is exact without-replacement math
