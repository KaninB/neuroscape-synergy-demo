How the numbers are calculated

The exact calculation sums all without-replacement card combinations whose printed faction-vector and non-RAM persistent count meet a tier. Dual-faction cards can supply both icons but only one board object.

P(draw requirement | h seen) = qualifying h-card combinations / C(deck size, h)
P(reached by turn t) ≈ simulated games with first hit by t / trials

The RAM target is chosen on separate pilot shuffles when “auto” is selected. The final evaluation uses independent shuffles. The structural simulator observes the hand but never the future deck order.
